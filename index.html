Blood, Bones & Sigils

Card numbers

Power is the damage dealt when a creature attacks. Health is how much damage it can take before dying. Damage remains between turns; the red heart shows current Health.

Draw

Your opening hand is dealt. On each later turn, choose the dependable Squirrel deck or Scout the creature deck: reveal the top three creatures, choose one, and place the other two on the bottom.

Blood

A red droplet marks a Blood cost. Offer creatures already on your side to pay it. Most provide one Blood; their deaths also leave Bones.

Bones

Every friendly death leaves one Bone. Cards with a Bone cost spend the pile without needing sacrifices. The Marrow Reserve starts trials I–III with 1, 2, and 3 Bones. Your first loss in a run grants one retry of that trial with 2 extra Broken Bones; a second loss ends the run.

Lanes & blocks

When you ring the bell, your creatures attack first from left to right. Normally each attacks the same numbered lane. An opposing creature blocks the hit and loses Health; an empty lane takes scale damage.

Tactics

You may use one tactical action per turn. Maneuver moves one creature into an adjacent empty lane. Mend spends 2 Bones to restore up to 2 Health without exceeding maximum Health. Moving and mending share the same opportunity, so choose carefully.

The contest

Your creatures attack first, but the opponent always replies before the round is judged. A trial cannot end before turn 12. From then on, a 20-point lead wins by domination. Otherwise, on turn 16 the leader wins regulation; a tie enters sudden death until one full round ends with a leader. If your Wolf deals 3 Power and their Stoat replies for 1 Power directly, you lead by 2.

Special targeting

Bifurcated Strike attacks the two diagonal lanes and not the forward lane. Trifurcated Strike attacks left, forward, and right. Airborne flies over an ordinary blocker unless Mighty Leap catches it.

Read the turn

Incoming cards are shown one turn early in their authored lane. If that lane is blocked, the opponent visibly flanks into the stronger adjacent lane when one is open; otherwise deployment fails. Compare Power, Health, and sigils before choosing a draw, sacrifices, and blockers. The Turn Ledger records every calculation.
