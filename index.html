The little rulebook

Blood, Bones & Sigils

Card numbers

Power is the damage dealt when a creature attacks. Health is how much damage it can take before dying. Damage remains between turns; the red heart shows current Health.

Draw

Your opening hand is dealt. On each later turn, choose exactly one unknown creature or one dependable Squirrel.

Blood

Red-seal cards require creatures already on your side to be offered. Most provide one Blood; their deaths also leave Bones.

Bones

Every friendly death leaves one Bone. Ivory-cost cards spend the pile without needing sacrifices. The Marrow Reserve starts trials I–III with 1, 2, and 3 Bones. Your first loss in a run grants one retry of that trial with 2 extra Broken Bones; a second loss ends the run.

Lanes & blocks

When you ring the bell, your creatures attack first from left to right. Normally each attacks the same numbered lane. An opposing creature blocks the hit and loses Health; an empty lane takes scale damage.

The contest

Your creatures attack first, but the opponent always replies before the round is judged. A trial cannot end before turn 8. From then on, a 12-point lead wins by domination. Otherwise, on turn 12 the leader wins regulation; a tie enters sudden death until one full round ends with a leader. If your Wolf deals 3 Power and their Stoat replies for 1 Power directly, you lead by 2.

Special targeting

Bifurcated Strike attacks the two diagonal lanes and not the forward lane. Trifurcated Strike attacks left, forward, and right. Airborne flies over an ordinary blocker unless Mighty Leap catches it.

Read the turn

Incoming cards are shown one turn early. Compare their lane, Power, Health, and sigils before choosing a draw, sacrifices, and blockers. The Turn Ledger records every calculation.
